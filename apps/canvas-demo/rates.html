What it assumed

    Betas are regressed from two years of daily observations, not typed in. A mapping below an R² of 0.2 is an assumption, and is marked as one. The history here is synthetic; the estimator is not.

    Forty legs across two names, each repriced on a 25×15 spot-vol grid against its own transmitted market. Curve, regressions and options all run in WebAssembly, in the same Rust the server uses.
